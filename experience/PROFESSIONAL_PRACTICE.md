### A repeatable approach to everyday support

The following describes a **general work method**, not a disclosed historical employer incident:

1. Record the user impact, device, symptoms and urgency in the support request.
2. Determine scope (one user, one workstation, several users, or a shared network/service).
3. Check the relevant client/device, account, configuration, connection or Microsoft 365 service within authorised access.
4. Apply an approved correction or escalate with evidence when the issue is outside the support role.
5. Verify with the user and document the resolution and closure.

**Examples of work categories:** workstation onboarding; Windows sign-in support; Outlook access and setup; hardware/software incidents; office network connectivity. Specific resolution steps in GitHub's simulated projects are training examples, not reproductions of confidential employer tickets.
